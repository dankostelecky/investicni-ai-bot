2. Mathematical & Analytical Framework

Relative Strength Index (RSI)

The RSI is the core momentum oscillator utilized for mean-reversion detection. It is calculated via the following formula:

RSI = 100 - [ 100 / ( 1 + RS ) ]

Where RS represents the ratio of average gains to average losses over 14 periods. The platform assumes a statistical lookback to optimize signal significance.

Data Metrics & Analysis Reference

Current Price: The last traded market price of the asset.

RSI (14): Momentum indicator showing if an asset is oversold (<30) or overbought (>70).

Profit / $1 Invested: Estimated return metric based on current mean-reversion analysis.

Verdict (Oversold/Entry): AI-generated signal indicating potential long entry based on RSI thresholds.

News Sentiment: Qualitative assessment of market news impact (Positive, Negative, Neutral).

Distance to 20d Peak: Mean distance from the asset's 20-day high, indicating retracement potential.

ATR Volatility (Average True Range): A measure of price variability; higher values indicate greater risk/reward potential.

Crowd Behavior: Analysis of retail volume and social sentiment.

Long-term Trend (SMA200): Comparison vs. 200-day Moving Average; crucial for identifying structural market shifts.

AI Learning Prediction: Machine learning model output forecasting future price targets.
